# Nostalgia

> Taste is the most stubborn memory of homesickness — the farther from home you go, the better that bowl of sanfan smells.

Jingyuan and Pingchuan sit on the upper reaches of the Yellow River, a fertile stretch of the Silk Road. Jingyuan has long been praised as <strong>"Jiangnan beyond the Great Wall"</strong>, a "famous town of Longshang" and "the pearl of the Yellow River"; the Yellow River runs 154 km through the county, the longest course of any county in Gansu. This rich land and water gave rise to a food culture all its own.

## The Three Great Hearty Dishes

Jingyuan is <strong>"the hometown of lamb"</strong> and the largest lamb-producing and distribution area in Gansu. Local food is built on wheat staples blended with coarse grains; the flavors run strong and the portions are generous. Three "hearty dishes" are renowned across Longyuan:

### 🐑 Jingyuan Lamb

"Famous beyond the northern frontier, celebrated across Longyuan." Jingyuan lamb is the first **China National Geographical Indication trademark** registered for a mammal: fine muscle fibers, high tenderness, a faint gamey note and a rich aroma. It can be stir-fried, braised in soy sauce, simmered in a yellow sauce, dry-fried, grilled, stewed plain or steamed, seasoned with more than ten kinds of Chinese medicinal herbs — every method is a specialty in its own right.

### 🐴 Dongwan Donkey Meat

The signature dish of Dongwan Town: bright in color, rich in flavor, and prepared by the rule of <strong>"one soak, two blanchings, three slow simmers"</strong>. It has a flavor all its own and is a must-try for any visitor to Jingyuan.

### 🥘 Sour Braised Pork

Also called "pickled cabbage and glass noodles stir-fried with pork", it began as a humble farmhouse dish. Shredded pickled napa cabbage is stir-fried with potato starch noodles and pork — sour and fragrant, cheap and delicious. Served with a bowl of yellow-millet sanfan, it is the most authentic taste of home. Today it has moved from the farmhouse stove to fine tables as a dish for guests — and it is the bite that every Jingyuan/Pingchuan native misses most after leaving home.

## Staples and Home-style Flavors

| Category | Examples |
|------|------|
| **Steamed** | steamed buns, flower rolls, matizi, mooncakes, baozi, fa gao, yellow-millet sweet momo |
| **Boiled** | long noodles (saozi noodles), jiejiezi, qihuazi, noodle slices, dumplings, **sanfan**, buckwheat jiaotuan |
| **Griddle-baked** | guokui (crisp outside, soft inside, easy to keep), large flatbread, **yi wo si** (thread-fine, crisp and fragrant) |
| **Fried** | youbing, youtiao, mahua, suhezi, sugar-oil cakes |
| **Cold noodles** | liangpi, a classic northwestern cold dish — smooth, chewy, a summer cooler |
| **Drinks** | guanguan tea, **jiangshui** (fermented celery water, cooling and detoxifying), laozao, sweet fermented rice, wheat tea, jujube tea |

### Seasonal Wild Vegetables

In spring and summer, sow thistle, wild chives, wild scallions, alfalfa, earth ear (Nostoc), wolfberry shoots, elm seeds and locust flowers appear in turn — nature's gift to this land.

### Banquets and Festivals

- **Weddings**: the "<strong>Thirteen Flowers</strong>" banquet is the norm — thirteen stir-fried dishes served in sequence, symbolizing good fortune and completeness
- **Festivals**: flower buns are colored with turmeric, fenugreek and red yeast and shaped like chrysanthemums, lotuses or Buddha's hands; known as "dim sum", they are a respectable gift when visiting relatives and friends

## Seven Geographical Indications

Jingyuan has **7 nationally protected geographical indication products** and was named one of the **first batch of national agricultural product quality and safety counties**:

> 🏅 Xiaokou jujubes · Jingyuan wolfberry · Jingyuan black melon seeds · Jingyuan lamb · Jingyuan yellowhorn oil · Jingyuan dry-sand watermelon · Damiao fragrant pears

It also holds titles such as "Hometown of Chinese Yellowhorn", "Hometown of Longyuan Vegetables", "Hometown of Wolfberry" and "Hometown of Seed Melons".
