# About Me

## Hometown: Pingchuan

I come from **Pingchuan District, Baiyin City, Gansu Province** — the little mining town that once belonged to Jingyuan.

![Cityscape of Pingchuan](https://www.bypc.gov.cn/upfiles/cms/20210417/1606112264194120.jpg)

### Origin of the Name

Pingchuan sits in central Gansu, on the upper reaches of the Yellow River, wedged into the middle of Jingyuan County. It takes its name from the **100,000-mu dry flat river plain** within its borders. It covers about 2,009 km², has a resident population of roughly 190,000, and administers 4 subdistricts, 5 towns and 2 townships; the district government is seated in Xingping Road Subdistrict. The climate is a typical temperate continental arid one, with an annual average temperature of 10.6 °C and only 195 mm of rain a year.

### Historical Evolution

Pingchuan was an important post station on the **Silk Road**, with a history that goes back to the Qin dynasty:

| Period | Milestones |
|------|--------|
| **Qin** | In the 33rd year of the First Emperor (214 BC), Meng Tian recovered the lands south of the Yellow River and built **Zhanyin City**, which belonged to Beidi Commandery |
| **Western Han** | In the 3rd year of Yuanding (114 BC), **Zhanyin County** was established — the earliest county-level administrative unit within present-day Baiyin; Zhanyin Pass became one of the earliest and most important Yellow River crossings on the Silk Road |
| **Western Wei** | Pingliang Commandery was renamed **Huizhou**, and Zhanyin became both the prefectural and commandery seat, turning it into a regional political, economic and cultural center |
| **Ming** | In the 2nd year of Zhengtong (1437), the Jinglu Garrison was established together with the **Dieliexun Inspection Office** |
| **Qing** | In the 12th year of Tongzhi (1873), Zuo Zongtang petitioned for the establishment of the **Dala'chi Sub-county** |
| **Modern** | On 14 May 1985, the State Council approved the restoration of Baiyin as a prefecture-level city; Baoji Town and five townships were carved out of Jingyuan County and **Pingchuan District was formally established** |

### A City of Mines and Industry

Pingchuan is a typical resource-based city. Its proven coal reserves reach **1.137 billion tonnes**, and its pottery-clay reserves exceed **4 billion tonnes**. Coal, electric power and ceramics are the three pillar industries, which earned it the name **"Porcelain Capital of the West"**. The Yinchuan–Lanzhou high-speed railway, the Beijing–Tibet Expressway and National Highway 109 all run through the district, so transport is convenient.

### Red Memories

- **1932**: the Shuiquan Uprising — the first armed uprising led by the Communist Party inside Gansu
- **October 1936**: the First and Fourth Front Armies **met at Dala'chi** and set up one of the earliest peasant-association revolutionary regimes in present-day Baiyin

### Sights and Culture

The district is home to a Ceramics Town (a national 3A-level scenic area), Yellow River rock carvings, the ruins of the Qin and Han Zhanyin City, the tomb of General Wang Jinbao, Beiwudang, the ancient village of Xiaohuangwan, and more.

## Present: Hangzhou

Today I live in Hangzhou and teach programming to children, teaching kids to build worlds with code.

Most of my spare time goes into tinkering with programming. The AI era has arrived, and there is far too much interesting stuff waiting to be explored. From the Loess Plateau to the water towns of Jiangnan, from a miner's kid to a programming teacher — the coordinates change, but the urge to tinker never does.
